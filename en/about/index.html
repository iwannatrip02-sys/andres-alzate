About me

I am from El Carmen de Viboral, the town of hand-painted pottery in eastern Antioquia, Colombia. I have carried a camera and a drone since 2018, and I build and fly my own FPV quads.

I have edited daily TV, concerts, documentaries, campaigns, festivals and podcasts. Almost three years at Viboral Televisión taught me to cut on deadline; the Instituto de Cultura festivals taught me to tell stories vertically.

I think in timelines and look at things like a painter. I want what I film to feel like it matters: the face of the person speaking, the light on stage, the road up to the village. That is why the blue of the pottery is the only colour on this site.
